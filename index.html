What is DwellKeep?

DwellKeep is an AI-powered household inventory app. Photograph a receipt and DwellKeep's AI reads it, extracts every item, and files it into your home inventory automatically — so when an insurance claim, warranty dispute, or move happens, your proof of ownership is one tap away.

Features

AI Receipt Scanning

Snap a photo of any receipt. Claude-powered vision AI extracts items, prices, and dates into structured inventory records — no manual typing.

Organized Home Inventory

Every item cataloged by room and category, with values, purchase dates, and receipt images attached.

Insurance-Ready PDF Reports

Generate a complete, professional PDF of your household inventory for insurers, warranty claims, or estate planning.

AI Home Assistant

Ask questions about what you own and get help pulling together the details for an insurance or warranty claim.

Cloud Backup

Sign in and your inventory, photos, and receipts sync to the cloud, so a lost phone doesn't mean lost records.

Free to Start

A free tier covers the basics. Premium unlocks unlimited items, rooms, reports, and AI use.

How it works

  1. Snap a photo of a receipt — or add items manually.
  2. AI extracts items, prices, and dates, and files them by room and category.
  3. Export an insurance-ready PDF report whenever you need proof.

Under the hood: one Flutter codebase shipping to iOS and Android. Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage) for accounts and sync. AI features run on the Anthropic Claude API through a server-side Cloud Functions proxy protected by Firebase App Check, so no API keys ship in the app. Subscriptions run on RevenueCat, iOS builds on Codemagic CI.
